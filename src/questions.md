## Question 01

Gamini Perera was allotted Lot A depicted in Partition Plan No. 2525 dated 2 February 1981, made by S. Silva, Licensed Surveyor, bearing Assessment No. 20, Flower Road, Homagama, containing in extent 10 perches, by a final decree of a partition case bearing No. 111/P dated 2 February 1985 entered in the District Court of Homagama.

Gamini Perera, by Deed of Gift No. 515 dated 10 March 1986, attested by R. Liyanage, Notary Public, gifted the said Lot A depicted in Plan No. 2525 absolutely and irrevocably to his son, Suresh Perera, subject to the life interest of Gamini Perera and his wife Vinitha. Gamini Perera died on 12 June 1990 due to heart failure. Vinitha revoked her life interest over the said Lot A depicted in Plan No. 2525 by Deed of Revocation of Life Interest No. 525 dated 25 February 1991, attested by S. Sapumal, Notary Public.

Suresh Perera married Kanthi and had two children, namely Rakitha and Muditha. Muditha died due to an accident on 5 June 2023, unmarried and issueless. Rakitha married Ann and died on 2 July 2022.

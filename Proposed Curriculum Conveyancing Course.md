## 1. Overview

This course aims to provide the skills required to qualify a law student as a
successful Notary Public in Sri Lanka.

This Professional Course in Notarial Practice (PCNP) is designed to fulfil the
required knowledge and relevant training requirements.

- **Duration:** One Year
- **Venue:** Sri Lanka Law College
- **Delivery Mode:** Blended (Physically and via Zoom)

### Course Framework

The course starts in beginning of January each year, and has 3 modules of study:

- **Module 1: Principles of Notarial Practice** (January – April)
- **Module 2: Applied Notarial Practice** (May – August)
- **Module 3: Internship** (September – December)

### Admission Pre-requisites

- Bachelor of Laws
- has passed Notaries Entrance Exam
- qualified as an Attorney-at-Law in Sri Lanka

### Course Content

This course consists of 3 Modules. Our flexible teaching approach has been
designed to enable practitioners to readily undertake the course.

The three modules are:

#### Module 1: Principles of Notarial Practice

The purpose of this module is to give students a theoretical foundation and
comprehension of the topics and concerns. Students should be able to show that
they comprehend the theoretical basis of notarial practice in Sri Lanka.

#### Module 2: Applied Notarial Practice

This module seeks to give students an in-depth, practical understanding of the
work of Sri Lankan notaries, including education on a notary's rights,
obligations, and duties as well as a thorough comprehension of notarial
statutes. After completing this module, students will be able to prepare legal
documents and recognize real-world issues that arise in everyday practice.

#### Module 3: Internship

This module provides the practical aspects of a Notary. To gain the necessary
hands-on experience, students are expected to serve as an intern or assistant
for a Notary with ten years of experience. After completing this module,
students are exposed to a trial period on Notarial practice. This is to ensure
they are well-versed in Notarial work.

### Learning Objectives

At the end of this module students should have a knowledge and understanding of:

- The function of a notary
- The notaries practice rules and regulations;
- How to prepare different notarial instruments;
- Understanding of notarial statutes;
- The law on the examination of title, drafting, execution, stamping, attesting
  and registration of deeds and notices;
- Capacity and incapacity;
- The notary's function in preventing frauds.

### Learning Outcomes

The course will give a thorough review of notarial statutes. It also covers the
practical elements of running a notarial business, such as keeping records and
upholding professional standards, as well as the professional rights,
obligations, and duties of a notary.

### Course Learning Outcomes

On successful completion of the Course, students should be able to:

- Critically review the theoretical underpinnings of notarial practice and
  contextualize current notarial practices in Sri Lanka.
- To assist residents, citizens, and organizations of Sri Lanka in managing
  their cross-border business and personal affairs.
- Offer creative and ethical solutions to practical and complex problems of
  notarial practice.
- Communicate coherently using effective interpersonal skills in professional
  practice.
- Understand notarial statues in Sri Lanka and a Notary's professional rights,
  obligations and duties.

## 5. Assessment Criteria

In order to become a Notary Public in Sri Lanka, students must pass assessments
for each module with a score of at least 50%.

- **Module 1: Short Answer Questions (50%)** — The Students will be assessed by a
  written examinations at the end of the second semester.
- **Module 2: Individual Assignments (20%)** — Ten (10) Assignments on the topics
  given in the list of assignments.
- **Module 3: Practical training — Internship Report (30%)** — Internship report
  from an Attorney-at-law who has been in active practice as a notary for a
  consecutive period of at least ten years.

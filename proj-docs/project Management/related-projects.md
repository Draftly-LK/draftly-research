# Related Projects

## Orbital Copilot's Title and Survey Workflow

- **Link:** <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWh5jXWs574>
- **Source:** YouTube video by Orbital
- **Type:** Real-estate/title workflow product demo

Orbital Copilot demonstrates a title and survey workflow for property-related work.
It is relevant to Draftly because it shows how legal/property professionals can use a
specialized assistant to work with title and survey documents instead of relying only on
general document search or manual review.

For Draftly, the useful comparison point is the workflow shape: uploaded property
documents, title/survey review, extracted case information, and a user-facing assistant
that supports professional decision-making. Draftly differs by focusing on Sri Lankan
legal drafting and review workflows, with conveyancing/title documentation as the first
use case.

## Lanka Law: New Law Reports

- **Link:** <https://lankalaw.net/case-laws/new-law-reports/>
- **Source:** Lanka Law
- **Type:** Sri Lankan legal research and case-law resource

Lanka Law provides access to Sri Lankan legal resources, including New Law Reports
volumes, Sri Lanka Law Reports, legislation, judgments, and related legal research
features. The New Law Reports page is useful as a reference point for how local legal
platforms organize reported case law and legal-resource search.

For Draftly, the comparison is mainly about scope. Lanka Law supports legal research
and access to legal materials, while Draftly focuses on client-specific document
drafting and review: uploaded deeds, assessment records, survey plans, extracted facts,
lawyer verification, and generated drafts.

## Harvey

- **Website:** <https://www.harvey.ai/>
- **Overview video:** <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ydIT4EdLK54>
- **Source:** Harvey
- **Type:** International legal AI platform

Harvey is an AI platform for legal and professional services. Its platform overview
shows how a legal AI product can support knowledge work across research, drafting,
analysis, and workflow assistance inside professional legal environments.

For Draftly, Harvey is useful as an international comparison for product positioning:
it demonstrates demand for AI-assisted legal work, but it is not designed around Sri
Lankan legal documents, local conveyancing practice, Sinhala/English mixed document
bundles, or lawyer-reviewed drafting from client-specific property records.

## Legora aOS

- **Link:** <https://legora.com/newsroom/legora-introduces-the-legora-aos-the-agentic-operating-system-for-legal-work>
- **Source:** Legora press release
- **Type:** International agentic legal AI platform

Legora introduces its aOS as an agentic operating system for legal work. The product
positioning is broader than a single drafting tool: it describes connected legal
workflows across matter intake, research, drafting, review, client delivery, document
management, email, and legal research systems.

For Draftly, Legora is useful as a comparison for workflow ambition. It shows how legal
AI products are moving from isolated task assistance toward connected legal-work
systems. Draftly is narrower and more local: it focuses on Sri Lankan legal drafting
and review, starting with client-specific document bundles and lawyer verification.

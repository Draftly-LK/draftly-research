Subsequent to a thorough analysis of the facts of this case, it could be revealed that the issues or the questions of law to be determined and decided is whether the 1st Respondent has a public duty to cancel the grant given to the 11th Respondent and to recommend the President of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka to cancel a grant given after land kachcheri or due process in terms of Land Development Ordinance, to the 11th Respondent. The other vital question of law is whether the Petitioner has a legitimate expectation with regard to cancellation of the grant given to the 11th Respondent and to recommend the President to cancel a grant given after land kachcheri. It could also be noted that Petitioner has complained in his Petition that the 11th Respondent has violated the terms and conditions of the relevant Grant to this application. The above Respondents deny the said violation of terms and conditions of the said Grant. Accordingly, the major facts and allegations are in dispute. It is held as follows in the case of THAJUDEEN VS SRI LANKA TEA BOARD AND ANOTHER (1981) 2 SLR 471 inter alia that; "Where the major facts are in dispute and the legal result of the facts is subject to controversy and it is necessary that the questions should be canvassed in a suit where Page 7 of 8 parties would have ample opportunity of examining the witnesses so that the Court would be better able to judge which version is correct, a Writ will not issue. Mandamus is pre￾eminently a discretionary remedy. It is an extraordinary, residuary and suppletory remedy to be granted only when there is no other means of obtaining justice. Even though all other requirements for securing the remedy have been satisfied by the applicant, the court will decline to exercise its discretion in his favor if a specific alternative remedy like a regular action equally convenient, beneficial and effective is available" In the circumstances aforesaid, the application is hereby dismissed, with cost Judge of the Court of Appeal Janak De Silva, J I agree.

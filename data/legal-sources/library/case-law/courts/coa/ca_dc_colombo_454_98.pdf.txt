S.Karunadhara for the Petitioner Anusha Nandasena with Mr.Palitha Mathew for the 3rd and 4th -Respondents 28.01.2013 Mr.Karunadhara who appears for the petitioner P.Sunil Fernando was directed on the last occasion to file substitution papers in order to substitute the heirs of the deceased plaintiff-respondent. He was directed to file papers, two weeks before today after having issued notice to the persons sought to be substituted. The appellant in this case is not being represented. It is the duty of the appellant to take necessary steps such as substitution to proceed with the appeal. The Registrar of this Court has sent notices on several occasions to the appellant directing him to be present in this Court but has failed to do so. Accordingly, it is clear that the appellant does not prosecute the appeal diligently. 1 In these circumstances it is evident that no steps have been taken to substitute the heirs of the deceased plaintiff-respondent either by the heirs of the deceased plaintiff-respondent or by the defendant-appellant. Therefore Court decides that the appellant is not prosecuting the appeal diligently. Accordingly, acting upon Rule 34 of the Supreme Court Rules, Court makes an order abating the appeal Accordingly the proceedings in this Court is terminated. Appeal abated and proceedings terminated .. JUDGE OFT KLP/- 2

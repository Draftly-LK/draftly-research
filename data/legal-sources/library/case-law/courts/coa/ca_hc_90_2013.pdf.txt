Counsel Neranjan Jayasinghe for the petitioner Hiranjan Pieris, SSC for AG Decided on 28.05.2015 H.N.J. Perera, J. Counsel for the Accused Appellant informs court that he will confines this appeal to the sentence imposed by the learned High Court Judge on the Accused Appellant. The Accused Appellant has been convicted for possession and trafficking of Heroin and found guilty on both counts. After conviction the learned High Court judge has sentenced him to death on both counts. It is submitted on behalf of the accused appellant that he has no previous convictions and that the accused is addicted to heroin. Counsel brings it to the notice of court that the accused in his dock statement too had stated so. Counsel moves that the court to consider the fact that there is no evidence to show that he is a habitual t I I • trafficker of heroin and therefore moves that the sentence imposed by the High Court Judge on the accused Appellant be converted to life imprisonment on both counts. After considering the facts and circumstances of this case, and the fact that the accused has no previous convictions, we set aside the sentence of death imposed by the learned High Court Judge on the Accused Appellant and substitute a term of life imprisonment on both counts. If for some reason, this life imprisonment imposed on the Accused Appellant is converted in to a fixed term, the Prison Authori ty is directed to implement the said sentence from the date of conviction namely 12.06.2013 Subj ect to the above variation of the sentence the appeal is dismissed. Appeal dismissed. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL K.K. Wickramesinghe, J. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL LA/-

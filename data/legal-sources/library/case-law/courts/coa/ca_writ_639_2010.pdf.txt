Nayomi Kahawita SC for the 3'd ,4th and 5th Respondents. 01.03.2013 ** Sriskandarajah, J (P.C/A) The Petitioner in this apparition seeking to quash the decision contained in document marked P14 directing the petitioner to provide re-employment of the 1st and 2"d respondents. It is brought to the notice of this Court that the Company is now closed and therefore the petitioner is unable to comply with the said direction contained in the document marked P14 dated 21.07.2010. In view of this fact the Commissioner, the 4th respondent is directed to vary the said order by making an order under section 6A of the Termination of Employment ( Special Provisions) Act. State Counsel undertakes to communicate this order to the Commissioner General of Labor. In view of this the proceedings of this application is terminated. President of the Court of Appeal Kpm/- 1

Relief will not be granted for default in prosecuting an appeal where - (a) the default has resulted from the negligence of the client or both the client and his attorney-at-law, (b) the default has resulted from the negligence of the attorney-at-law in which event the principle is that the negligence of the attorney-at-law is the negligence of the client and the client must suffer for it.

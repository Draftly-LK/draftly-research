IN THE COURT OF APPEAL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA Case No. CA 653/98 (F) DC Kuliyapitiya 11142/L Ilanganathilakage Premachandra Jayasinghe No: 01/30, Kurunegala Road, Narammala. Petitioner Vs. B.K. Steaphen Nikalas No: 28, Kurunegala Road, Narammala. Respondent ( Case No. CA 653/98 (F) DC Kuliyapitiya 11142/L BEFORE K.T. Chitrasiri, J. COUNSEL Parties are absent and unrepresented. DECIDED ON 18.01.2013 K. T. CHITRASIRI, J. This is an appeal seeking to set aside the order dated 1 7. 09. 1998 delivered by the learned District Judge of Kuliyapitiya. In that order, learned District Judge allowed an application by the defendant and vacated the judgment delivered upon an ex-party trial. In that order learned District Judge has considered the circumstances under which the defendant was absent on the day the case was taken up for trial. She has analyzed carefully, the reasons for the absence. I am unable to find any wrong, in the way the evidence to purge the default had been considered. Accordingly, this Court is not inclined to interfere with the order made on 17.09.1998. For the reasons stated above, the appeal is 1 dismissed. Accordingly the order made on 17.09.1998 by the learned District Judge allowing the defendant to file answer would stand as it is. Learned District Judge is directed to proceed with the case from that point onwards. Appeal dismissed with costs. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL KRL/- 2

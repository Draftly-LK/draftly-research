The Petitioner in this application is seeking a writ of certiorari to quash the order contained in the letter dated 01.06.2012 marked P3 and a writ of mandamus directing and/ or compelling the 01 st Respondent to hold an inquiry and to make a final order on the appeal submitted by the Petitioners contained in P4. The appeal that was filed which is marked as P4 dated 28.06.2012 was submitted by an Attorney-at-Law and not by the parties concerned. The Section 60(2) of the Co-operative Society Ordinance states that the parties affected could prefer an appeal to the Registrar of Co-operative Society . The Registrar of the Co-operative Society by his letter dated 19.07.2012 marked P5 has replied to the Attorney-at-Law who has submitted the said appeal and informed that the persons mentioned in paragraph 1 of the appeal preferred by the Attorney-at-Law could prefer an appeal as they have the right to make an appeal to the Registrar of Co-operative Society and in that event he has said that he could consider the appeal and respond. 2 This Court is of the view that the Attorney-at-Law who prepared the appeal would have got the relevant parties to sign the appeal instead of he himself sending it in his name, as there is no procedure laid down in this regard. In the above circumstances, as no proper appeal submitted by the aggrieved party to the Registrar of Co-operative Society according to the Registrar of Co-operative Society he has not considered the said appeal. In these circumstances, as the Petitioner has not placed a prima facie case before this court for issuance of notice, Court refuses issue notice. P.W.D.C. Jayathilake I agree.

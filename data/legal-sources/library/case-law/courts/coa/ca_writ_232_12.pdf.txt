However, it appears to this Court that in terms of sub paragraph (b) of the prayer to the petition, the petitioner has sought a writ of mandamus as well as a writ of certiorari. This Court also notes that inquiry that is to be held by the Mahawali Authority should afford ~n hearing to the petitioner as well as to the 4th respondent. On that basis, learned Counsel for the petitioner moves to withdraw this application. The application for withdrawal is allowed and the application IS dismissed.

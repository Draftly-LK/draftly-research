Sriskandarajah, J. (PICA) COUNSEL : Sanjeewa Ranaweera for the Petitioner. ARGUED& DECIDED ON : 11.03.2013. S.Sriskandarajah, J. (PICA) Heard Counsel for the Petitioner. Counsel for the Petitioner in this application is seeking a writ of certiorari to quash a charge sheet issued on 31.07.2012 marked P7. The charge sheet is issued under the Establishment Code and any challenge on the authority to issue charge sheet or any irregularity on the charge sheet has to be raised before the inquiring officer in the 1st instance and if he makes an order or he fails to make an order the petitioner according to law could appeal to the Public Service Commission. But in this instance Petitioner has not sought the intervention of Public Service Commission but he is seeking a writ of certiorari to quash the said charge sheet. As the petitioner has an alternative remedy by way of challenging any jurisdictionally issue before the PHC and as he has not sought ,.,-./ 1 that alternative remedy he is not entitled to challenge the same in this Court. As such this court refuses to issue notice on the Respondent. PRESIDNENT OF THE COURT OF APPEAL. NRI￾2

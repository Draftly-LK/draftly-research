For the Respondent. 28.02.2014 Both parties heard on the application. Learned State Counsel has no objection to the suspect been granted bail. The fact that the suspect had pending cases as stated by the learned High Court Judge, will have no bearing on the application for bail as admittedly a charge of murder cannot be maintained on the evidence available. According to the J .M.O.'s report the cause of death is stated to be Alcoholic Liver disease.

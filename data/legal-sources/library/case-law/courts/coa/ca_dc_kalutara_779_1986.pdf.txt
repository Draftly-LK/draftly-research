Heard Counsel in support of the application for substitution. The 1 st Defendant-Appellant Mohamed Lebbe Abdul Rasak has died on 26.04.1990. Death certificate has been produced marked 'A'. He has died leaving 6 children as heirs of his estate. The petitioner is one of the said 6 children. The petitioner has produced the birth certificates of the said children marked A, B, C, D, E and G. I have perused all required documents. I am of the view that the petitioner is a fit and proper person to be substituted in place of the deceased 1 st Defendant-Appellant. Hence I order to substitute Abdul Razak Mohamed Nalir alias Haleel as the Substituted 1 st Defendant-Appellant. Registrar is directed to amend the caption accordingly. Tender amended caption. The learned Counsel for the 1 s\ 10th, 11th, 2 12th and 14th Defendant-Appellants makes an application to withdraw this appeal. Application for withdrawal is allowed. Appeal is dismissed without costs. Registrar is directed to send the main record to the relevant District Court. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL AKN

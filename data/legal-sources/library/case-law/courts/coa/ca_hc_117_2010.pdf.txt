All four accused-appellants are present in court produced by the prison authorities. Heard submissions of the Counsel for the 2nd, 3rd and 4th accused-appellants. Counsel inform court that only evidence against the said appellants were identification evidence. Counsel for the appellants make submissions that these appellants were shown to the witnesses by the police on several times, prior to the identification parade. The Counsel submit that the police by doing so had acted illegally. The State agrees that, only evidence against the 2nd , 3rd and 4th appellants was the identification evidence, which the State also 1 I I I I I I ! admit as flawed. Therefore, it is unsafe to convict these appellants on this evidence. Therefore Court allows the appeal of the 2nd, 3rd and 4th accused-appellants. The 2nd accused-appellant namely Locukatagodage Don Chaminda Jayanath, 3rd accused-appellant namely Paththini Kuttige Sarath Nonis and 4th accused-appellants namly Warnakulasuriya Jood Nelson Fernando are acquitted and discharged from the charges. The appeal against the 1st accused-appellant is postponed for further date as the Counsel is not present before Court. The Commissioner of Prison is informed that the 2nd , 3rd and 4th accused-appellants have been acquitted and discharged from the charges. Appeal against the 1st accused-appellant is fixed for argument on 28/02/2013. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL Depali Wijesundera, J. I agree. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL. /mds 2

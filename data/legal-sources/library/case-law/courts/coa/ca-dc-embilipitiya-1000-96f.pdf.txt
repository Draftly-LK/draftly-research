By the said impugned judgment, the learned district judge dismissed the partition action inter alia for failure to establish adequate details regarding the original owner of the subject matter. Even though the plaintiffs maintained in the plaint that the original owner of the corpus was one Hendrik Appuhamy, as is evident from P2 (deed No 617) produced by the plaintiff himself the original owner are Sirisena, Dingihamy and Hinnihamy. As there was a serious confusion arising from the deeds produced by the plaintiffs themselves the learned district judge was not able to investigate the title set out in the plaint and therefore dismissed the partition action.

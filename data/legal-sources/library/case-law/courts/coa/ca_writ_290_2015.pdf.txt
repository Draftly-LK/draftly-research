Malalgoda PC J Petitioners to the present application have come before this court seeking inter alia, prerogative writs in the nature of Writ of Certiorari and Writ of Prohibition. a) To quash a decision to promote 18th to 11 i h Respondents to the grade of Officer Trainee b) To prohibit 1st to 15th Respondents from promoting to the grade of Officer Trainee contrary to the provisions contained in P-6 and P-lO In addition to the above relief, the Petitioners have prayed for the issuance of an interim relief directing the 1st to 1 i h Respondents not to confirm 18th to 11 i h Respondents in the Grade of Executive Officer in the 1st Respondent. When the matter was supported before this court for notices, the Learned President Counsel who represented the 1st to 17th Respondents (except 2R) informed court that the issuance of interim relief had become academic since 18th to 11 i h Respondents have already being confirmed in the Grade of Executive Officers but did not object for the issuance of notice on the Respondents. Mter notices were issued on the Respondents, the Learned Counsel for the Petitioners once again renewed their application for interim relief and the matter had come up before us on 27.01.2016 to support for interim relief. I I I r I [ I I • ! I I ! 8 At the time the matter was supported before us for interim relief 15t and 3rd to 1 i h Respondents through their counsel raised a preliminary objection, to the effect that the interim relief prayed in the Petition is wider, than the main relief prayed for. As pointed out by the Learned Counsel for the 15t to 1 i h Respondents (except 2 R) the main relief prayed by the Petitioners refer to the quashing of a decision to promote the 18th 11 i h Respondents by way of a Writ of Certiorari and Prohibiting such promotion by way of a Writ of Prohibition but no relief is sought by way of Writ of Certiorari or Prohibition to quash a decision to confirm or prohibit any confirmation of the said Respondents. When considering the material placed by both parties before this court including the confirmation letters of several Respondents filed along with a motion dated 18th December 2015 the court observes that, a large number of Respondents have now confirmed in their positions by the 15t Respondent bank and therefore granting interim relief will become purely academic and of no use. It is further observed by this court that in the absence of main relief, preventing the confirmation of the 18th to 11 i h Respondents and also considering the fact that several of them have now confirmed in their position, this court decides to up hold the preliminary objection raised by the Respondents. Preliminary objection up held. Interim relief not granted. PRESIDENT OF THE COURT OF APPEAL s. Devika de. L Tennakoon J I agree, JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL

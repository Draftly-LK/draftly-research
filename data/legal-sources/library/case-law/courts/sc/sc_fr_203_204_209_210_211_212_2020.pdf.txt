The Inspector General of Police in his Affidavit1 has taken up the position that the National Police Commission is empowered to grant special promotions to police officers as per the document dated 13-03-2020 marked 1 R1. The operative parts of that document are contained in paragraph 3 of that document. Interestingly, the Petitioners have also relied on this document and indeed the Petitioners have also produced this document marked P14A with their Petition and with their counter Affidavit dated 25-11-2021. According to 1 R1, while the National Police Commission is empowered to grant special promotions to police officers, it must be noted that such special promotions must be strictly decided on the criteria set out in that document. The grounds on which such special promotions could be granted by the National Police Commission are set out in paragraph 3 of that document. I observe that there are three parts identified as (a), (b) and (c) under that paragraph. Paragraph 3(a) deals with situations where the relevant police officer has either got himself killed in the course of his duty or has become disabled to the extent mentioned therein. Paragraph 3(b) of 1 R1 deals with situations in which the relevant police officers had rendered a significant and commendable duty in the course of his duties pertaining to investigations of crime. Paragraph 3(c) of 1 R1 deals with situations where relevant police officers have obtained achievements in sports, a significant dedication during distress risking their lives, and occasions where they had performed exceptional duty in the police service. I also observe few important and relevant observations in 1 R1. They are set out below; I.

# Conveyancing Case-Law Topic Index

## Purpose

The curriculum asks students to prepare a case-law index for each conveyancing
topic. This file gives Draftly a controlled starting structure without copying
entire law-report databases or relying on isolated summaries.

## Use in Draftly

Do not treat this as a completed case digest. Each case used in a title report
or legal memo must be saved to the relevant case folder with its full citation,
court, year, legal issue, source URL, and lawyer approval.

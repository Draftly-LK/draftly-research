## Cross-cutting findings the workers inherit

- Manifest hashes were not reproducible across checkouts (CRLF). Fixed
  centrally: `scripts/package_integrity.py`, `hash_mode` per file, all 77
  legacy entries migrated, verifier and tests added.
- No tree schema or validator existed. `scripts/validate_finalized_trees.py`
  added; one hard error on the opening run (Apartment Ownership amendment
  27 of 2002 has an empty `amends`).
- Files under `library/amendments/` are matched to statutes by number-year
  alone. Confirmed mismatches: `28-2018-medical-amendment.pdf` (Medical Act),
  `11-1963-marriage-registration-amendment.html` (not the Finance Act),
  `36-1981-civil-law-amendment` (Civil Law Ordinance, not CPC),
  `39-1988-grant-of-citizenship`, `32-2002-monetary-law`,
  `16-1986-vidyaranya-vinayakrama-sabhawa-incorporation`,
  `17-2002-survey` (the Survey Act itself), `23-2018-apartment-ownership`
  (filed under Stamp Duty), `22-1955-administrative-districts` (genuine
  omnibus, in nine chains).
- `data/processed/amendment-chains.csv` reads only the first header page,
  so it stops early for CPC (14 of 1993) and Municipal Councils (48 of 1971).
- 35 registry rows point `local_markdown_path` at files that do not exist.

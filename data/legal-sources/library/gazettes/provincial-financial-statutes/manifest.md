# Provincial Financial Statutes and Stamp-Duty Gazettes

## Purpose

Provincial stamp-duty rules are jurisdiction-specific and change through
provincial financial-statute amendments and gazette notices. This collection is
an index for selecting the law that applies to the property's province and the
transaction date; it is not a single nationwide statute.

## Western Province Baseline

- [Financial Statute of the Western Province Provincial Council, No. 6 of 1990](../../statutes/western-province-financial-statute/western-province-financial-statute-6-1990.pdf)
- [Western Province Department of Revenue Financial Statute page](http://www.revenuedept.wp.gov.lk/en/?p=18211)
- [Financial Statute Amendment, No. 4 of 2014](../../amendments/western-province-financial-statute/western-province-financial-statute-amendment-4-2014.pdf)
- [Financial Statute Amendment, No. 1 of 2016](../../amendments/western-province-financial-statute/western-province-financial-statute-amendment-1-2016.pdf)

## Selection Rule

For each matter, record the province, instrument date, and transaction type.
Retrieve the applicable province's current statute, amendments, rate notices,
and procedure from its official provincial authority or the Department of
Government Printing. Preserve the selected source URL and gazette number in
the case manifest.

## Verification Note

The Western Province baseline and two later amendments are stored locally. The
Western Province material must not be treated as the stamp-duty rule for
property outside the Western Province.

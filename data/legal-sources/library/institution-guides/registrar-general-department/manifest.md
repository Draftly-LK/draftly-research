# Registrar General's Department Guides and Forms

## Purpose

This collection records RGD service pages and available public forms relevant to
notarial practice, deed registration, title registration, powers of attorney,
and land registry searches.

## Use in Draftly

Treat these as procedural and form references, not as statutory authority. For
case work, save the actual form used in the case folder with the completion
date and any receipt or submission reference.

Wills (Amendment)
AN ACT TO AMEND THE WILLS ORDINANCE

BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows :

[18th February , 1993 ]
Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Wills (Amendment) Act, No. 5 of 1993.

Amendment of section 3 of Chapter 60.

2. Section 3 of the Wills Ordinance is hereby amended by the substitution for the words " No will made by any male under the age of twenty-one years, or by any female under the age of eighteen years,", of the words "No will made by any person under the age of eighteen years,".

Sinhala text to prevail in case Inconsistency.

3. In the event of any inconsistency between the Sinhala and Tamil texts of this Act, the Sinhala text shall prevail-

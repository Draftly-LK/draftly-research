Trusts (Amendment)

AN ACT TO AMEND THE TRUSTS ORDINANCE.
BE it enacted by the Queen's Most Excellent Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Senate and the House of Representatives of Ceylon in this present Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows: -
Short title.
1. This Act may be cited as the Trusts (Amendment) Act, No. 7 of 1968.
Amendment of section 21 of Chapter 87.
2. Section 21 of the Trusts Ordinance is hereby amended, by the substitution, for all the words from " to preclude, in any case ", to the end of that section, of the words " to preclude the deposit of trust money in a Government Savings Bank (including the Ceylon Savings Bank). "

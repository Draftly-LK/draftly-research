Civil Law (Amendment)
AN ACT TO AMEND THE CIVIL LAW ORDINANCE.
Act Nos,
36 of 1981
[29th May , 1981 ]
Short title.

1. This Act may be cited as the Civil Law (Amendment) Act.

Amendment of section 5 of Chapter 79.

2. Section 5 of the Civil Law Ordinance is hereby amended by the substitution for the words five per centum", of the words "twelve per centum ".(* This change has been already made in the Revised Edition to bring it into line with Sections 5, 6 and 7 of the Civil Procedure Code (Amendment) Act, No. 53 of 1980.)

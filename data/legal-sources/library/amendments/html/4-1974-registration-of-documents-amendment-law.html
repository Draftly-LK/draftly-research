Registration of Documents (Amendment) Law

A LAW TO AMEND THE REGISTRATION OF DOCUMENTS ORDINANCE.
BE it enacted by the National State Assembly of the Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:-
Short title.
1. This Law may be cited as the Registration of Documents (Amendment) Law, No. 4 of 1974.
Repeal of section 2a of the Registration of Documents Ordinance.
2. Section 2A of the Registration of Documents Ordinance (inserted therein by Act No. 27 of 1969) is hereby repealed.

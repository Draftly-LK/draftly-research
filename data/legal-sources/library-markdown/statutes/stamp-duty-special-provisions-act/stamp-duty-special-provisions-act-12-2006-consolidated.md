Stamp Duty (Special Provisions) Act No 12 of
                   2006

This consolidated text of the Stamp Duty (Special
Provisions) Act No 12 of 2006 incorporates the amendment
made to that Act by the Stamp Duty (Special Provisions)
(Amendment) Act No.10 of 2008.

Please note, however, that this consolidated text cannot be
regarded as a statutory copy of the Stamp Duty (Special
Provisions) Act No 12 of 2006. It is prepared for the
purpose of convenient reference only.

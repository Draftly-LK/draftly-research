# Topic 16: Local Authority, UDA, and Other Regulations

## Purpose

Local authority ownership registration, survey-plan approvals, UDA approvals,
planning clearances, and area-specific by-laws or gazettes.

## Source IDs

| Source ID | Role |
| --- | --- |
| SRC039 | Urban Development Authority Act. |
| SRC041 | Town and Country Planning Ordinance. |
| SRC042 | Urban Councils Ordinance. |
| SRC061 | Municipal Councils Ordinance. |
| SRC062 | Pradeshiya Sabha Act. |
| SRC063 | Local authority by-laws and gazettes. |

## Status

The UDA, Urban Councils, Municipal Councils, and Pradeshiya Sabhas sources are
downloaded as public consolidated PDFs. Town and Country Planning plus
area-specific by-laws and gazettes remain in the source backlog.

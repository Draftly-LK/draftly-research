# Topic 17: Drafting of Deeds

## Purpose

Drafting deed types such as transfers, condominium transfers, executor's
conveyances, administrator's conveyances, gifts, mortgage bonds, leases,
agreements to sell, wills, powers of attorney, exchanges, declarations,
rectifications, and partitions.

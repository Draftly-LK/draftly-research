# Topic 20: Criminal and Civil Liabilities of Notaries

## Purpose

Civil and criminal liability provisions affecting notaries across stamp duty,
financial statutes, apartment ownership, Buddhist temporalities, fragmentation,
land reform, land alienation, land development, notarial practice, and title
registration.

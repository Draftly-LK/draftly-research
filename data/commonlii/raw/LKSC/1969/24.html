Thameena v. Koch - NLR - 192 of 72 [1969] LKSC 24; (1969) 72 NLR 192 (6 May 1969)

192

1969 Present : Tennekoon, J.

Mrs. M. U. THAMEENA, Appellant, and J. H. KOCH, Respondent

S. C. 112/67-L. T. 1/18051

Labour Tribunal-Order made by it-Incapacity of Supreme Court to deal with it by way of revision.

The revisionary powers of the Supreme Court do not extend to revision of orders made by Labour Tribunals.

APPEAL from an order of a Labour Tribunal. R. L. Jayasuriya, for the appellant. T. Parathalingam, with C. Sandrasagara, for the respondent.

May 6, 1969. TENNEKOON, J.-

Mr. Parathalingam objects to this appeal being heard on the ground that it is out of time. Mr. Jayasuriya appearing for the appellant concedes that the appeal is out of time by four days. He however moves that the matter be dealt with in revision. This I am not prepared to do as I do not think the revisionary powers of this Court extend to orders made by Labour Tribunals.

The appeal is rejected with costs.

Appeal rejected.

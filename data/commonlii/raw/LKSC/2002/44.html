Prevention of Terrorism Act - Remand of suspect until conclusion of trial - Section 7(1) proviso - Duty to inform Attorney-General of fact of arrest and detention.

400

Court observed:

 

It is a necessary implication of the proviso to section 7(1) of the Prevention of Terrorism Act that the authorities responsible for the arrest and detention of a sus�pect must inform the Attorney-General as soon as possible of the fact of such arrest and detention to review the matter and determine whether he should be fur�ther detained in custody.

APPLICATION for relief for infringement of fundamental rights.

V. Yogeswaran for petitioner.

Viveka Siriwardena., State Counsel for respondents.

Hearing fixed for 15.03.2002

January 24, 2002

 

The respondents are granted time till 25.02.2002 to file objec�tions. The petitioner is granted two weeks time thereafter to file his counter affidavit. Hearing on 15.03.2002.

 The petitioner in this case was arrested on 19.05.2001 and was produced before the Magistrate of Vavuniya on 02.07.2001 in case No. B419/2001. Apparently the petitioner was remanded until the conclu�sion of the trial. However, until leave was granted in this application, no steps were taken to have the petitioner brought to trial, nor have steps been taken to ascertain whether the Attorney-General consents to the release of the petitioner from custody in terms of the proviso to section 7(i) of the Prevention of Terrorism Act.

 
In court's view, it is a necessary implication of that proviso that the authority responsible for the arrest or detention must inform the Attorney-General as soon as possible of the fact of such arrest and detention in order to enable the Attorney-General to review the matter and to determine whether he should be further detained in custody.

 
Court thinks it is desirable that that procedure will be followed in all the other cases.

Jeremiah Et Al v. Rapielpillai Et Al - NLR - 143 of 47 [1946] LKCA 4; (1946) 47 NLR 143 (14 February 1946)

143

1946 Present: Soertsz S.P.J.

JEREMIAH, et al., Appellants, and RAPIELPILLAI, et al.,
Respondents.

184- C.R. Mallakam, 12,317.

Appeal- Duty of appellant to furnish Court with typescript copies of all necessary documents.

An appellant should see that the typescript copies prepared for the Court of Appeal contain all the documents necessary for a consideration of his appeal.

APPEAL from a judgment of the Commissioner of Requests, Mallakam.

                   N. Nadarajah, K.C. (with him H. W. Thambiah and N. Kumarasingham) for the plaintiffs, appellants.

                  S. Nadesan (with him N. Nadarasa), for the defendants, respondents.

Cur. adv. vult.

144

February 14, 1946. SOERTSZ S.P.J.-
   
The appellants have preferred an appeal in a case regarding a right of way, and have not condescended to place before me the two most important documents in the case, namely P1 and P2. They probably expect me to look at the original record to acquaint myself with maps and plans. This I refuse to do. It is for appellant to see that the typescript copies prepared for the Court of Appeal contain all the documents necessary for a consideration of his appeal.
   
I see no reason to disturb the findings of the commissioner.
   
I dismiss the appeal with costs.

Appeal dismissed.

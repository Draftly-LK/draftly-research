Civil Procedure Code - Sections 75, 75(6) General denial of averments in plaint - Is it a denial contemplated under section 75(d) ?

HELD:

(1) There is no reference either denying or admitting the averments in paragraphs 6/7 of the plaint. Nowhere does the defendant-petitioners in their answer admit averments in paragraphs 2-7, 12 and 17 of the plaint, there is no denial of the averments therein other than a general denial in paragraph 1 in the answer.

(2) Where a defendant does not deny an averment in the plaint - he must be deemed to have admitted that averment.

Per Somawansa, J. :

"Section 75 which deals with the requirements of an answer does not contemplate a general denial of the averments in the plaint but requires a statement admitting or denying the several averments in the plaint."

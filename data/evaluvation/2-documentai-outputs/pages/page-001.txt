Question No.01
Gamini Perera was allotted Lot A depicted in Partition Plan No 2525 dated 2nd February 1981 made by S
Silva Licensed Surveyor bearing assessment No 20 Flower Road, Homagama containing in extent 10
perches by a final decree of a partition case bearing No 111/P dated 2nd February 1985 entered in the
District Court of Homagama.
Gamini Perera by Deed of Deed of Gift No. 515 dated 10th March 1986 attested by R Liyanage Notary
Public gifted said Lot A depicted in plan. no 2525 absolutely and irrevocably to his son Suresh Perera
subject to life interest of Gamini Perera and his wife Vinitha. Gamini Perera died on 12th June 1990 due
to heart failure. Vinitha revoked her life interest over said Lot A depicted in Plan No. 2525 by Deed of
revocation of life interest bearing No. 525 dated 25th February 1991 attested by S Sapumal Notary Public.
Suresh Perera married Kanthi and had two children namely Rakitha and Muditha. Muditha died due to
an accident on 5th June 2023 unmarried and issueless. Rakitha married Ann and died on 2nd July 2022.
Suresh Perera died on 10th December 1995 intestate. Suresh Perera had an offspring named Samantha
from his first marriage who is a citizen of USA (United States of America).
(i) Draw up the pedigree showing the devolution of title from Gamini Perera up to the present owners
in relation to the land and premises bearing Assessment No. 20 Flower Road, Homagama. (15 marks)
(ii) What are the shares presently owned by each heir in the property?
(4 marks)
(iii) Can Samantha become entitled to the intestate estate of Suresh Perera? Discuss with the provisions
of Land (Restriction on alienation) Act No. 38 of 2014 as amended by Land (Restriction on alienation)
(Amendment) Act No. 21 of 2018.
(2 marks)
(iv) Explain the rules set out in sections 22 to 26 of Matrimonial Rights and Inheritance Ordinance No.
15 of 1876.
(4 marks)

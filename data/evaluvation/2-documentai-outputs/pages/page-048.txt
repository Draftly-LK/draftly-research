Lot B together with a Reservation for a Road marked Lot R of the Land called Dewatagahawatta in
extent of 30 perches situated in Rajagiriya.

observe that Lot B and R depicted in the said Survey Plan are sub-divisions of Lot 1 containing in

Deed of Transfer No. 234 dated 23rd March 2015 attested by Walter de Silva Notary Public sold
Lot B together with undivided 1/3rd share of the Road Reservation marked Lot R to Sunil Peiris.

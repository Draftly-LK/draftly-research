## 2. Issues identified

Gamini Perera was allotted Lot A in Partition Plan No 2525 (2 February 1981, S Silva LS), assessment No 20 Flower Road, Homagama, 10 perches, by the final decree in partition case No 111/P of the District Court of Homagama dated 2 February 1985.

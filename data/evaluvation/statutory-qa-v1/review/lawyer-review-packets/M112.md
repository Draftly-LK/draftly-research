### Draft answer

Issue: Whether Lal Peiris, a notary whose warrant authorises him to practise in Colombo and who is a friend of the purchaser, may attest a deed transferring land situated in the Matara District, and what he must do in order to attest it lawfully.

Rule: A transfer of immovable property is of no force in law unless the deed is in writing and attested by a licensed notary (Prevention of Frauds Ordinance s.2). The area limit on a notary is imposed by Notaries Ordinance s.31 rule (22), which forbids him to authenticate or attest any deed "in any area other than that in which he is authorized to practice", and to attest in, or to attest a deed drawn in, a language other than that in which he is authorised to practise. The restriction is on the place at which the notary performs the attestation and on the language of the deed; rule (22) says nothing about where the land affected by the deed is situated. The area in which he is authorised to practise is fixed by his warrant, extended by Notaries Ordinance s.4A, under which a warrant authorising practice in the judicial division in which he resides is deemed to authorise practice in the judicial zone in which he resides; s.9 requires him to keep his office within the area specified in the warrant. The only disqualification in s.31 arising from the notary's own relationship to the deed is rule (13), which forbids him to attest a deed to which he is a party. Before attesting, he must have an assurance that the required stamp duty is provided (s.31 rule (6)), and the deed must embody an accurate and clear description of the land showing its boundaries, extent and situation, specifying the district (s.31 rule (16)(a)).

Application: Lal Peiris's warrant authorises him to practise in the Sinhala language in Colombo, the judicial division in which he resides, and by s.4A that authority extends to the Colombo judicial zone. The deed is drawn in Sinhala, and the vendor, the purchaser and the two witnesses are to sign it together in his presence at his Colombo office on 12 March 2018. The act of attestation is therefore performed within the area and in the language in which he is authorised to practise, and rule (22) is satisfied. That the land lies at Kumbalwella in the Matara District does not engage rule (22), because that rule looks to the area in which the notary acts and not to the situation of the land; the Matara District instead has to be stated in the description of the land under rule (16)(a). Lal Peiris is not the vendor or the purchaser, takes nothing under the deed and has no interest in the property, so rule (13) is not engaged; his friendship with Waruna Liyanage is not a matter on which s.31 fastens. He must also satisfy himself under rule (6) that the stamp duty is provided, and Waruna Liyanage has undertaken to put him in funds before the day of signing.

Conclusion: Lal Peiris may attest the deed. He must do so within the Colombo judicial zone, which his warrant covers by virtue of Notaries Ordinance s.4A, and in the language in which he is authorised to practise, so the parties and the two witnesses must execute the deed before him at his Colombo office and not at Matara (s.31 rule (22)). The situation of the land at Matara is no bar to his attesting; it must, however, be described in the deed with its district under s.31 rule (16)(a), and he must not attest until he has an assurance that the stamp duty is provided (s.31 rule (6)). His friendship with the purchaser does not disqualify him, since he is not a party to the deed (s.31 rule (13)).

## M112-Q02 (enrich, hop count 2)

What is the duty cast on Lal Peiris regarding this Deed as the land is situated outside the District in which he resides?

### Draft answer

Issue: What duty is cast on Lal Peiris by reason of the land being situated in the Matara District, which is a district other than the Colombo District in which he resides.

Rule: Every notary must, under Notaries Ordinance s.31 rule (26)(a)(i), deliver or transmit to the Registrar of Lands of the district in which he resides, so as to reach the registrar on or before the fifteenth day of every month, the duplicate of every deed or instrument (except wills and codicils) executed or acknowledged before or attested by him during the preceding month, together with a list in duplicate signed by him in the prescribed form. Where the deed is one other than a will or codicil and affects land situated in a district other than that in which the attesting notary resides, s.31 rule (29) casts an additional duty on him: on or before the fifteenth day of the month next following that in which the deed was executed, and besides transmitting the duplicate in the manner already described, he must deliver or transmit to the Registrar of Lands of the district in which the land is situated a copy of the deed certified by him as correct, together with a list in duplicate in the prescribed form signed by him of all such deeds as relate to lands in that district. The district of the land is the district which s.31 rule (16)(a) requires to be specified in the description of the land in the deed itself. Breach of a rule set out in s.31 is an offence punishable on summary trial before a Magistrate, in addition to any civil liability the notary incurs (s.34).

Application: Lal Peiris resides in the Colombo District and attests the deed at his Colombo office on 12 March 2018. The deed is an ordinary deed of transfer, not a will or codicil, and he is the only notary before whom it is executed, so the duty of transmitting the duplicate rests on him. The land conveyed is at Kumbalwella in the Matara District, a district other than the Colombo District in which he resides, so s.31 rule (29) is engaged. His ordinary duty under rule (26)(a)(i) is to send the duplicate of the deed, with the signed list in duplicate, to the Registrar of Lands, Colombo, so as to reach that registrar on or before 15 April 2018. Rule (29) then requires him, in addition and by the same date of 15 April 2018, to deliver or transmit to the Registrar of Lands, Matara, a copy of the deed certified by him as correct, together with a list in duplicate signed by him of the deeds he has attested that relate to lands in the Matara District. The deed must in any event describe the land as being in the Matara District under rule (16)(a), which is what identifies the registrar to whom the certified copy must go.

Conclusion: Because the land lies in the Matara District and Lal Peiris resides in the Colombo District, s.31 rule (29) of the Notaries Ordinance casts on him the additional duty of delivering or transmitting to the Registrar of Lands, Matara, on or before the fifteenth day of the month next following execution, that is on or before 15 April 2018 for a deed executed on 12 March 2018, a copy of the deed certified by him as correct together with a list in duplicate in the prescribed form signed by him of the deeds relating to lands in that district. This is over and above, and does not replace, his ordinary duty under s.31 rule (26)(a)(i) to transmit the duplicate and his monthly list to the Registrar of Lands, Colombo, by the same date. Failure to observe the rule is an offence under s.34 of the Ordinance in addition to any civil liability he may incur; the fine applicable in April 2018 cannot be stated from the corpus, which carries only the later text of s.34.

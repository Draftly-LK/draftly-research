### Draft answer

Issue: What share of Lot B, and of the undivided one-third share of the road reservation Lot R, does each surviving heir of Sunil Peiris hold, he having died intestate and childless on 6 December 2016 leaving a widow, a surviving mother, two full sisters, two paternal half-brothers and one maternal half-brother?

Rule: Inheritance ab intestato to immovable property in Sri Lanka is governed by the Matrimonial Rights and Inheritance Ordinance (s.21). The surviving spouse inherits one-half of the intestate's property (s.22), and subject to that right inheritance passes in the order descendants, ascendants, collaterals (s.23), descendants being preferent to all others (s.24). Where children and remoter descendants fail and only one parent is alive, the surviving parent takes half and the full brothers and sisters of the deceased, together with the half-brothers and sisters related to the intestate by the side of the deceased parent, take the other half (s.25). Where the qualifying half-siblings are all of one side, the full siblings first take one-half of that portion and divide the remaining half with the half-siblings (s.27), those equally near in degree taking per capita (s.30).

Application: Sunil Peiris died intestate on 6 December 2016 leaving no children, so the descendant class fails. His widow Kusum Peiris takes one-half of Lot B and of the undivided one-third share of Lot R under s.22. His father Charles Peiris had predeceased him and his mother Pushpa Peiris was alive, so under s.25 Pushpa takes half of the remaining half, that is one-fourth. The other one-fourth goes to the full sisters Kanthi and Manel, both children of Charles and Pushpa, together with Upul and Wimal, who are half-brothers related to Sunil by the side of the deceased parent Charles. Keerthi Perera is the child of Pushpa by her earlier marriage to Lal Perera and is therefore related to Sunil only through the surviving parent, so s.25 does not admit him. Because the qualifying half-siblings are all on the father's side, s.27 gives Kanthi and Manel one-half of that one-fourth, that is one-eighth or one-sixteenth each, and the remaining one-eighth is divided per capita under s.30 among Kanthi, Manel, Upul and Wimal, that is one thirty-second each.

Conclusion: The present heirs hold Lot B, and the undivided one-third share of Lot R, as follows: Kusum Peiris, the widow, one-half; Pushpa Peiris, the mother, one-fourth; Kanthi three thirty-seconds; Manel three thirty-seconds; Upul one thirty-second; Wimal one thirty-second. Keerthi Perera takes no share. The six shares exhaust the whole, so all six must join in any conveyance of the entirety of Lot B to the purchaser.

### Draft answer

Issue: What fractions of premises Assessment No. 3/2B, being Lot 5B in Plan No. 225, are presently held by each of the persons who take through Lalith Perera, who died intestate on 2 March 2018 leaving his wife Kamani and his children Hasini and Kushan, Kamani having afterwards gifted her share to her three children including Ruvini, her daughter by an earlier marriage?

Rule: Inheritance ab intestato to immovable property in Sri Lanka is governed by Part III of the Matrimonial Rights and Inheritance Ordinance No. 15 of 1876 (s.21), which applies to the estate of a person who dies after the proclamation of the Ordinance and who, if married, married after it (s.20). A surviving spouse inherits one-half of the intestate's property (s.22). Subject to that half, descendants are preferent to all others and all the children take equally per capita (ss.23 and 24). A woman governed by the Thesawalamai needs her husband's written consent only for a disposition made during the marriage (Jaffna Matrimonial Rights and Inheritance Ordinance No. 1 of 1911 s.6), and on the death of a spouse only the deceased spouse's thediatheddam is divided, the survivor's own acquisition remaining hers (s.20).

Application: Mala Nadaraja bought Lot 5B for value on 1 August 2014 from Malika Peiris, who was then 20 years old, so that link is unaffected by the minority that spoils the Lot 5A branch. Lot 5B was Mala Nadaraja's own acquisition and none of it devolved on Mohan Nadaraja's heirs at his death, and when she executed Deed of Transfer No. 789 on 5 April 2017 she was a widow, so no written consent was required and Lalith Perera took the whole of Lot 5B. On his intestate death on 2 March 2018, Kamani as surviving spouse took one-half, and the remaining one-half went to Hasini and Kushan in equal shares, one-fourth each. Ruvini is Kamani's daughter by an earlier marriage and not a descendant of Lalith Perera, so she inherited nothing from him. Kamani then gifted her one-half to Hasini, Kushan and Ruvini, giving each of them a further one-sixth. Hasini's total is one-fourth plus one-sixth, that is five-twelfths, and Kushan's is the same; Ruvini holds one-sixth, or two-twelfths; and Kamani, having given away her whole half, retains nothing.

Conclusion: Premises Assessment No. 3/2B is presently owned as to five-twelfths by Hasini, five-twelfths by Kushan and one-sixth, that is two-twelfths, by Ruvini. Kamani retains no share. Ruvini's interest comes wholly from Kamani's gift and not from any right of inheritance in the estate of Lalith Perera.

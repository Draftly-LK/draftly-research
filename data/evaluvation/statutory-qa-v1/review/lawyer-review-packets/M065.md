## Original background

Anil Fernando is a Notary practising in Colombo. A company named Cyril & Cyril (Private) Limited wishes to lease out a warehouse situated in Galle. The company wants Anil Fernando to attest its Lease Agreement. Anil Fernando knows the directors of the said company but he does not know the owner of the warehouse, namely Mr. Russel Perera. Mr.Russel Perera wants his Notary Ruwan Perera who is practising in Galle to draft and attest the Lease Agreement. Russel Perera does not want to come to Colombo.

## M065-Q01 (keep, hop count 3)

### Question

How can they execute this Lease Agreement?

### Legal issues

- ISS-M065-Q01-01: Whether one lease agreement may be executed before two different notaries when the parties will not sign at the same time and place.
- ISS-M065-Q01-02: Whether a notary authorised to practise in Colombo may attest a signature given in Galle, or the reverse.
- ISS-M065-Q01-03: How a private limited company signs a lease that the law requires to be notarially attested.
- ISS-M065-Q01-04: What the notary who did not draft the deed must have endorsed on it before he attests it.

  Excerpt: "(25) Where any deed or instrument is executed or acknowledged before more than one notary- (a) the notary who first attests such deed or instrument shall comply with all the requirements of rule (20), and every other notary attesting such deed or instrument shall comply with the requirements of paragraphs (a) to (e) of the aforesaid rule and the provisions of paragraph (g) in respect of erasures, alterations and interpolations made in the signatures attested by him or in his serial number;"
  Why: This is the provision that expressly permits one deed to be executed or acknowledged before more than one notary and allocates the attestation duties between the notary who attests first and every other attesting notary. Temporal: Rule (25) has not been touched by Notaries (Amendment) Act No. 47 of 2011, No. 13 of 2013, No. 31 of 2022 or No. 6 of 2024, none of which lists rule (25) among the rules amended. The text was therefore in force at October 2020.
- PROV-M065-002 Notaries Ordinance No. 1 of 1907 s.32((2)) [verified] corpus `1-1907/s32`
  Excerpt: "In the case of any deed or instrument which is to be executed by two or more parties, both or all of whom, as the case may be, do not sign the deed or instrument at the same time and place- (i) the deed or instrument shall, for the purposes of the application of rules (6), (7), (23) and (25) set out in section 31, be deemed to be executed or acknowledged at the time when it is first signed by a party, or by two or more parties at the same time and place;"
  Why: Section 32(2) is the statutory recognition that the parties to one deed may sign at different times and in different places, and it fixes the deemed date of execution for the purposes of rule (25). - PROV-M065-005 Notaries Ordinance No. 1 of 1907 s.31(rule (12)) [verified] corpus `1-1907/s31`
  Excerpt: "He shall not authenticate or attest any deed or instrument unless the person executing the same and the witnesses shall have signed the same in his presence and in the presence of one another, and unless he shall have signed the same in the presence of the executant and of the attesting witnesses."
  Why: Rule (12) forbids attestation of a signature not made in the notary's presence. ### Draft answer

Issue: Whether, and by what procedure, a lease of a warehouse in Galle can be executed when the corporate party wants its Colombo notary to attest for it, the owner wants his Galle notary to draft and attest, and the owner will not travel to Colombo.

Rule: A lease of immovable property other than a lease at will or one not exceeding a month affects an interest in land and must be in writing and notarially attested (Prevention of Frauds Ordinance s.2(1)). A notary may not attest a deed in any area other than that in which he is authorised to practise (Notaries Ordinance s.31 rule (22)), and may not attest unless the executant and the witnesses have signed in his presence and in the presence of one another and he has signed in their presence (rule (12)), with at least two witnesses subscribing their signatures in letters (rule (8)). The Ordinance nevertheless allows one deed to be executed or acknowledged before more than one notary: the notary who attests first must comply with all of rule (20), every other attesting notary must comply with paragraphs (a) to (e) of rule (20) and with paragraph (g) as to erasures in the signatures he attests and in his serial number, each notary numbers the deed in his own series under rule (23), the first notary keeps the protocol under rule (24) and every other notary keeps a certified copy as his protocol (s.31 rule (25)). Where the parties do not sign at the same time and place, the deed is deemed executed for the purposes of rules (6), (7), (23) and (25) when it is first signed (s.32(2)(i)). The duplicate goes to the Registrar of Lands of the district in which the first attesting notary resides, and the other notary need not send one (rule (28)). A notary may not attest a deed drawn by another person unless a certificate signed by the notary who drew it is endorsed on the deed (rule (2)). A company enters an obligation that must be notarially attested by signing under the company's name by two directors, or by its sole director, or by a person authorised by its articles, or by an attorney appointed by it, and being notarially executed (Companies Act s.19(1)(a)).

Application: Neither notary can take both signatures, because rule (22) confines Anil Fernando to Colombo and Ruwan Perera to Galle, and rule (12) forbids either of them from attesting a signature not made in his presence. Section 31 rule (25) read with section 32(2) removes the difficulty: the lease can be executed in two stages before two notaries. Ruwan Perera drafts the lease in Galle and endorses on it his certificate under rule (2) that he drew it, which is what allows Anil Fernando to attest it later. Russel Perera signs in Galle before Ruwan Perera in the presence of two witnesses who also sign; Ruwan Perera attests there and then as the first notary and, as first notary, must satisfy the whole of rule (20). The deed is then taken to Colombo, where the company signs under its name in the manner permitted by Companies Act s.19(1)(a), before Anil Fernando and two witnesses; Anil Fernando attests as the second notary and needs to comply only with rule (20)(a) to (e) and, as to erasures in the signatures he attested and in his serial number, rule (20)(g). Each notary gives the deed a number in his own consecutive series; Ruwan Perera keeps the protocol and Anil Fernando supplies himself with a certified copy as his protocol. Identification is satisfied on each side by the notary who knows that party, since Ruwan Perera is Russel Perera's own notary and Anil Fernando knows the directors. Because Ruwan Perera attests first and resides in the same district as the warehouse, he transmits the duplicate to the Registrar of Lands of the Galle district under rule (28) and no separate certified copy to another district's registrar arises under rule (29).

Conclusion: They can execute the lease as one deed signed at different times and places before two notaries under section 31 rule (25) of the Notaries Ordinance read with section 32(2): Ruwan Perera drafting in Galle, certifying that he drew it, and attesting Russel Perera's signature there as the first notary, and Anil Fernando attesting the company's signature in Colombo as the second notary. Russel Perera therefore does not have to come to Colombo. The order could be reversed, but taking Ruwan Perera first keeps the protocol and the duplicate with the notary who resides in the district where the warehouse lies. This is a proposed statement of the statutory position and is unverified.

Issue: How did Nimal's half share of divided lots 6 and 7 devolve on his intestate death and on the successive intestate deaths of his widow Nalini and his son Bimal, so that a pedigree from Nimal to the present owners can be drawn?

Rule: Intestate succession to immovable property in Sri Lanka is governed by the Matrimonial Rights and Inheritance Ordinance s.21(1), the lots being immovable property within s.3. The Ordinance applies here because none of the exclusions in s.2 (Kandyan, Muslim, Thesawalamai) is disclosed, and the s.20 condition is met since every death and marriage in question post-dates the 1876 proclamation. On each intestacy the surviving spouse first takes one-half of the estate (s.22). Subject to that half, the right of inheritance runs in the order descendants, ascendants, collaterals (s.23). Children take equally per capita and the issue of a deceased child take per stirpes (s.24), and heirs equally near in degree take per capita (s.30). Where descendants fail, the estate goes to the parents (s.25); where both parents have also failed, it goes to the brothers and sisters, whether of the whole or half blood (s.26). Where the half-siblings are on one side only, s.27 directs that the full siblings take one half of that portion first and divide the other half with the half-siblings.

Application: Step 1 - Nimal's holding. Under the Final Decree of 21 August 1996 Nimal was allotted 5/12 of lots 6 and 7, and by Deed of Transfer No. 346 of 6 June 1998 he bought Aruni's 1/12. At his death he therefore held 5/12 + 1/12 = 6/12, that is a half share of the land. Sunil's 5/12 and Kusum's 1/12 are untouched by the deaths and remain with them. Step 2 - Nimal's intestacy, 23 April 2008. He left his widow Nalini and four children: Achini (by his first wife Malini), Amal, Bimal and Krishni. Nalini takes one-half of his estate under s.22, which is 1/4 of the land. Under ss.23, 24 and 30 the other half, also 1/4 of the land, is shared equally per capita among the four children, giving each child 1/16 of the land. Step 3 - Nalini's intestacy, within a year of April 2008. Her estate is the 1/4 she took from Nimal. Nimal had predeceased her, so there is no surviving spouse to take under s.22, and the whole estate passes to her descendants under ss.23, 24 and 30. Her children are Amal, Bimal and Krishni only; Achini is Nimal's daughter by Malini and is not a descendant of Nalini, so she takes nothing from this estate. Each of the three takes 1/4 divided by 3, that is 1/12 of the land. Immediately after this Amal, Bimal and Krishni each hold 1/16 + 1/12 = 7/48 of the land and Achini holds 1/16. Step 4 - Bimal's death, 4 March 2010, married to Amali and issueless. On the footing that he too died intestate, his estate of 7/48 devolves as follows. Amali takes one-half under s.22, that is 7/96 of the land. The remaining 7/96 must pass down the s.23 order: Bimal left no descendants, and under s.25 it would go to his parents, but both Nimal and Nalini were already dead, so under s.26 it goes to his brothers and sisters of the whole and half blood. Amal and Krishni are of the full blood; Achini is a half-sister on the father's side and there is no half-sibling on the mother's side. Section 27 therefore applies in its final limb: Amal and Krishni first take one half of the 7/96, that is 7/192, in equal shares of 7/384 each, and the other 7/192 is divided per capita among Amal, Krishni and Achini under ss.27 and 30, giving each of them 7/576. So from Bimal's estate Amal takes 7/384 + 7/576 = 35/1152, Krishni takes the same 35/1152, Achini takes 7/576 = 14/1152 and Amali takes 7/96 = 84/1152.

Conclusion: The pedigree runs: Nimal (holder of 6/12 of lots 6 and 7, being 5/12 under the Final Decree of 1996 plus 1/12 bought from Aruni by Deed No. 346 of 1998) died intestate on 23 April 2008 leaving his widow Nalini and four children, Achini (by his first wife Malini), Amal, Bimal and Krishni; Nalini took one-half of his estate and the four children the other half in equal shares. Nalini then died intestate within a year, and her share passed in equal shares to her own three children Amal, Bimal and Krishni, Achini being excluded as not her descendant. Bimal, married to Amali, then died on 4 March 2010 issueless and, on the footing that he died intestate, his share passed one-half to Amali as surviving spouse and the other half to his siblings, both parents having predeceased him: his full siblings Amal and Krishni taking one half of that portion between them and sharing the remaining half with his paternal half-sister Achini. The present owners of Nimal's half share are therefore Amal, Krishni, Achini and Amali, alongside the original co-owners Sunil and Kusum who are unaffected.

### Draft answer

Issue: Whether a notary who is instructed by his employer to execute a Deed of Transfer urgently may draw and attest that deed without searching the land registry to ascertain the state of the title, and what follows under the Notaries Ordinance if he does.

Rule: Notaries Ordinance s.31 makes it the duty of every notary strictly to observe the rules set out in that section. Rule (17)(a) of s.31 provides that before any deed or instrument, other than a will or codicil, affecting any interest in land or other immovable property is drawn by him, the notary shall search or cause to be searched the registers in the land registry to ascertain the state of the title in regard to such land and whether any prior deed affecting any interest in such land has been registered. Rule (17)(b)(i) requires him to obtain from the Registrar of the relevant Land Registry a certified extract of the folio containing the last entry showing the ownership of and the registered encumbrances on the land. The proviso to rule (17) creates the single exception: if the parties to the transaction authorise the notary in writing to dispense with the search, the search is not compulsory, but he must then write at the head of the deed the reference to the previous registration, if any, before tendering it for registration. Rule (33) adds that on a deed transferring immovable property the notary shall use his best endeavours to obtain the title deed. Breach of rule (17) is an offence: it is not among the rules listed in s.34(1)(a) or (b), so it falls within s.34(1)(c) and carries, on summary trial before a Magistrate, a fine not exceeding fifty thousand rupees in addition to any civil liability. Section 33 saves an instrument from invalidity only where the notary's non-compliance is in respect of a matter of form; it does not address the notary's own liability.

Application: The instrument is a Deed of Transfer of an allotment of land at Talangama North, so it affects an interest in land and is not a will or codicil, and rule (17)(a) applies to it before the deed is drawn, not merely before it is registered. What the employer has given Saman is a photocopy of the vendor's 2014 deed. That is neither a search of the registers in the land registry nor the certified folio extract required by rule (17)(b)(i), so on these facts nothing has been done to ascertain the state of the title or to discover whether any prior deed affecting the land has been registered. The proviso cannot be relied on, because neither the vendor nor the purchaser has authorised Saman in writing to dispense with the search; the employer is not a party to the transaction, and its instruction is in any event oral. The duty under s.31 lies on Saman in his capacity as notary. His employment as a legal officer of the real estate company does not shift that duty to his employer, and the Ordinance recognises no exception for urgency. Whether a deed executed in breach would nonetheless be a valid instrument is a separate question, since s.33 operates only where the omission is a matter of form; that point is open on these facts and does not affect Saman's own position.

Conclusion: No. Saman cannot execute the Deed of Transfer without checking the title at the land registry. Rule (17)(a) of s.31 of the Notaries Ordinance obliges him to search or cause to be searched the land registry registers before he draws the deed, and rule (17)(b)(i) obliges him to obtain the certified folio extract. Because neither party has given him written authority to dispense with the search, the proviso to rule (17) does not apply, and his employer's urgency is no answer. If he proceeds he is guilty of an offence under s.34(1)(c), liable to a fine not exceeding fifty thousand rupees in addition to any civil liability. The proper course is to search the registers and obtain the certified folio extract first or, if the parties are willing, to take their written authority to dispense with the search and then write the reference to the previous registration at the head of the deed before tendering it for registration.

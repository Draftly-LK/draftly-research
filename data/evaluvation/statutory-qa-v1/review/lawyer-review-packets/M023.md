### Draft answer

Issue: What fractional shares in Lot 1 are presently held by each heir, after the intestate deaths of Kapila on 24 January 2011, of Anusha on 15 June 2012 and of Prema on 4 September 2015.

Rule: Intestate succession to immovable property in Sri Lanka is governed by Part III of the Matrimonial Rights and Inheritance Ordinance (s.21(1)), which applies to the estate of any person dying after its proclamation (s.20). A surviving spouse first takes one-half of the intestate's property (s.22). Subject to that, inheritance passes in the order descendants, ascendants, collaterals (s.23), children taking equally per capita and the issue of a deceased child taking per stirpes (s.24). If descendants fail and only one parent survives, the surviving parent takes half and the full brothers and sisters, together with half-brothers and sisters related through the deceased parent, take the other half (s.25). If both parents have failed, the estate goes to the brothers and sisters, whether of the whole or half blood (s.26); where all the half-siblings are related on one side only they take the whole, unless an ascendant on the other side survives (s.29). Heirs equally near in degree take per capita (s.30). The word 'unmarried' means not having a husband or wife living (s.3).

Application: Kapila held the entirety of Lot 1 under Deed of Gift No 925 once his father's reserved life interest ended on Mahesh's death on 24 April 2008. On Kapila's intestate death on 24 January 2011 his widow Champa took one-half (s.22) and his four surviving children Anusha, Prema, Rupa and Seetha took the remaining half equally per capita under s.24, that is 1/8 each. Anusha died on 15 June 2012 issueless, leaving her husband Hasitha Perera, who took one-half of her 1/8, that is 1/16 (s.22). Her descendants having failed and both her parents Kapila and Sandya having predeceased her, the balance 1/16 passed to her siblings under s.26; her only siblings were the three half-sisters Prema, Rupa and Seetha, all related to her on her father's side alone, and no ascendant on her mother's side is shown to survive, so under s.29 they took the whole 1/16, per capita under s.30, that is 1/48 each. Each of Prema, Rupa and Seetha then held 1/8 + 1/48 = 7/48. Prema died on 4 September 2015 unmarried (s.3) and issueless, so no spouse's half is deducted under s.22 and no descendant takes under s.24. Her father Kapila had predeceased her and her mother Champa survived, so under s.25 Champa took half of 7/48, that is 7/96, and the other 7/96 went to her sisters. Her only surviving siblings were her full sisters Rupa and Seetha, Anusha having already died issueless, so they took 7/192 each under s.25 read with s.30. Section 27 does not alter Anusha's devolution because she left no full siblings, so the half-blood division there is governed by s.29.

Conclusion: The present shares in Lot 1 are: Champa Gunesekera 55/96 (that is 110/192), being 1/2 inherited from Kapila plus 7/96 inherited from Prema; Rupa Gunesekera 35/192; Seetha Gunesekera 35/192; and Hasitha Perera 12/192, that is 1/16, as the widower of Anusha. These fractions total 192/192. The computation assumes, as the facts do not state otherwise, that Anusha and Prema each died intestate and that no ascendant on Sandya's side survived Anusha. Malani and Mithila take nothing in this land because Mahesh had already gifted the entirety away in 2006.

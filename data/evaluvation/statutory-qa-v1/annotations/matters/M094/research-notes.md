# M094 research notes (status=unverified)

Matter: codicil to Last Will No. 3250; exam session April 2019. Two Tier A
questions, both `keep`, so no enrichment was applied.

## Queries tried

- `acts`, then `toc 21-1844` and `toc 1-1907`.
- `search "codicil"` surfaced Prevention of Frauds Ordinance ss.4-13 and Wills
  Ordinance ss.6 and 9, which carried Q01.
- `show 1-1907/s31 --full`, then `show 31-2022/s16` and `show 6-2024/s3` to work
  out which rules of s.31 changed after April 2019.
- `grep "form E" --act 1-1907` and `search "attestation form second schedule
  notary"`: only the 2022 replacement Form E is in the corpus.
- `grep "will" --act 12-2006` for stamp duty on a codicil: no match, not pursued.

## Alternatives rejected

- Notaries Ordinance s.38 (true consideration of a sale) and s.38A (inserted in
  2024): not engaged.
- Registration of Documents Ordinance: wills and codicils sit outside the
  duplicate and registration regime in s.31 rules (26) to (29).
- Wills Ordinance s.5 (foreign wills) and ss.7-8: not engaged on these facts.

## Corpus gaps

- The Second Schedule to the Notaries Ordinance, and so Form E as it stood in
  April 2019, is absent.
- Section 31 exists only as the post-2022/post-2024 consolidation. Rules (9),
  (20)(b) and (20)(h) all changed after the matter date. The April 2019 text of
  rule (9) survives only as a recital inside 31-2022/s16, which is why that
  amending section is recorded as a provision.
- Prevention of Frauds Ordinance s.4 exists only in its post-2022 form.
- Notarial precedent forms and the Roman-Dutch law of the life interest are not
  statutory and are not in the corpus.

## For the verifier

- Q01 is `reserved` (`mixed_statute_and_case`) because the marks turn on drafting
  form; revisit if the benchmark treats drafting form as statutory.
- Q02 is `blocked_temporal_uncertainty` though the authority requirement is
  `statute_only`; check the drafted attestation against the 2019 edition.
- Rules (8), (11), (12), (15), (18), (20)(a),(c),(d),(f), (21), (22) and (23) are
  treated as unchanged since before 2019 on the strength of the amendment lists
  in 31-2022/s16 and 6-2024/s3. Confirm that reading.

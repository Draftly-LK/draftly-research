# M036 research notes

Paper 6, question 2 (October 2023). Tier A: Q01 (steps on a purchase of a
41-acre rubber estate by a private company) and Q02 (documents to call for on
title to an adjoining 40-perch residential premises). Q03 is Tier B.

## Queries tried

`acts`; `toc` on 38-2014 and 2-1958; `show` on 2-1958/s3, s14, s25 and
20-2005/s10; 38-2014/s2, s4, s18, s25; `search` in 7-2007 for major
transactions, capacity and execution, then 7-2007/s2, s19, s185; `search` in
23-1927 for priority and for searches/copies, then 23-1927/s7, s11, s32, s42;
`search` in 1-1972 for the ceiling and for transfer restrictions,
`grep "public company" --act 1-1972`, then 1-1972/s3, s5, s8, s66;
`show 1-1907/s31 --full` with 31-2022/s16 and 6-2024/s3 to date each rule;
7-1840/s2, 30-2022/s2, 4-2024/s2, 22-1871/s3; `search` in 21-1998 for the
certificate of title; `grep` for "street line", "building line", "non-vesting".

## Alternatives rejected

- Mortgage Act No. 6 of 1949: the encumbrance point is carried by the
  registration priority rule and the folio-extract duty.
- Civil Procedure Code testamentary sections (s.518, the s.554 series):
  considered for a devolution-by-death limb in Q02 and dropped, because no
  available section states a clean "call for probate" rule.
- Municipal Councils Ordinance s.69/s.70 and Urban Councils Ordinance s.74:
  placing this rural parcel inside a Municipal or Urban Council area would
  have been an implausible added fact.
- Land Reform Law s.42A onward (public companies): the purchaser is a private
  company, so s.8 is the operative route.

## For the verifier

1. 2-1958/s25. Corpus body is the as-enacted "one hundred acres"; Act No. 20
   of 2005 s.10(b) substituted "twenty hectares" for "forty hectares" and the
   corpus records that as `substitute_not_applied`. 41 acres is below both
   thresholds, so the conclusion holds either way; check the wording.
2. Whether Q01 and Q02 are properly `statute_only`. Both answers are confined
   to steps and documents with a statutory anchor; local-authority
   certificates and the thirty-year root convention are recorded as gaps.
3. Execution formalities are dated: Prevention of Frauds Ordinance s.2 is
   cited through Act No. 30 of 2022 s.2, because the corpus body is
   consolidated to 2024 and the transferee-signature subsection came in by Act
   No. 4 of 2024. Only pre-2024 rules of Notaries Ordinance s.31 are used.
4. CL-M036-Q01-014 is `inferential`: clearing a registered mortgage is an
   inference from 23-1927/s7 rather than its literal terms.

## Rejected

Prevention of Frauds Ordinance s.2 (execution, not schedule content or deed
construction); Revocation of Irrevocable Deeds of Gift Act No. 5 of 2017
(postdates the deeds); Registration of Title Act No. 21 of 1998 (title
registration, not the deed system these deeds sit in); Survey Act No. 17 of 2002
and Surveyors Ordinance (no bearing on the schedule).

## Corpus gaps

- No statutory rule of construction of deeds, and `nemo dat quod non habet` is
  not enacted anywhere in the corpus. Q03 turns on the conflict between the
  recital, operative clause and schedule of Deed No. 409, so it is `reserved`.
- Notaries Ordinance s.31 is consolidated to 2024, so its rule (16) wording as at
  April 2018 cannot be confirmed. Supporting only, `applicable_to_matter_date`
  false. The Pradeshiya Sabha Act No. 15 of 1987 is absent, so one limb of rule
  (16)(a) cannot be read in full.

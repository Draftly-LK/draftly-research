## Alternatives rejected

- Kandyan Succession Ordinance, Jaffna Matrimonial Rights and Inheritance
  Ordinance, Muslim Intestate Succession Ordinance: nothing in the facts brings
  the Peiris family under any of them, so the general law in 15-1876 applies.
- Prescription Ordinance s.3: no possession facts are given, so it adds nothing.
- Registration of Title Act No. 21 of 1998: the land is on the deeds register at
  the Colombo Land Registry, not on a title register.
- Municipal Councils / Urban Councils / Urban Development Authority provisions on
  street lines and non-vesting: real conveyancing practice, but the corpus cannot
  tell us which authority covers Rajagiriya, so nothing was recorded.

1. Q02 turns on reading s.27 into the collateral half created by s.25. Only the
   paternal half-siblings qualify under s.25, so s.27's one-side limb was applied:
   Kanthi 3/32, Manel 3/32, Upul 1/32, Wimal 1/32, with Kusum 1/2 and Pushpa 1/4.
   A reading that gave all four siblings an equal 1/16 is arguable and would change
   the numbers; this is the single most contestable step in the map.
2. Three provisions carry post-matter-date amendments and were deliberately kept
   out of the indispensable sets: 7-1840/s2 (2022, 2024), 1-1907/s31 (2022, 2024)
   and 2-1889/s205 (2023). Confirm the pre-amendment wording before relying on them.
3. Q03 names four document classes. The choice of four is conveyancing practice;
   each one's statutory hook is cited, but the framing is not itself statutory.

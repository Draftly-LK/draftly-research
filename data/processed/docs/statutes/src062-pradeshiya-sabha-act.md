# Pradeshiya Sabha Act

Pradeshiya Sabhas

AN ACT TO PROVIDE FOR THE ESTABLISHMENT OF PRADESHIYA SABHAS WITH
A VIEW TO PROVIDE GREATER OPPORTUNITIES FOR THE PEOPLE TO PARTICIPATE
EFFECTIVELY IN DECISION-MAKING PROCESS RELATING TO ADMINISTRATIVE
AND DEVELOPMENT ACTIVITIES AT A LOCAL LEVEL; TO SPECIFY THE POWERS,
FUNCTIONS AND DUTIES OF SUCH SABHAS; AND TO PROVIDE FOR MATTERS
CONNECTED THEREWITH OR INCIDENTAL THERETO.


BE it enacted by the Parliament of the Democratic Socialist Republic of Sri Lanka as follows:

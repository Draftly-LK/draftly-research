# Conveyancing Institution Source Pack

## Purpose

This manifest maps the curriculum's institution-duty section to official public
service pages or source locations.

## Case Handling Rule

Institution guides are workflow sources. In a real matter, Draftly should still
record the relevant office, district, local authority area, form version,
submission date, receipt number, and lawyer verification status.

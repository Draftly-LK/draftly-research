The proceedings in this matter were terminated on August 4, 2015. The present application arises from the motion filed by the Petitioner, E. Jude Marco Perera, along with the petition and affidavit dated April 21, 2023. The 7th Respondent, who is now deceased, is the sole Respondent named in the said application. The relief sought by the Petitioner is primarily based on the alleged violation of bail conditions by the 7th Respondent, which is said to have indirectly affected the Petitioner's rights. The Petitioner, by the motion filed along with the petition and affidavit dated 22 November 2023, seeks the relief prayed for in the Petition dated April 21, 2023, subject to necessary amendments. In the prayer to the Petition dated April 21, 2023, the reliefs sought are primarily against the 7th Respondent. However, as the 7 th Respondent is now deceased, this Court is unable to grant any relief sought against him. The other reliefs prayed for in this Petition can be accommodated within the reliefs prayed for in SC/FR/323/2024 and SC/Contempt/04 insofar as they are just, equitable, and within the scope of the jurisdiction of this Court. Therefore, the present action before this Court is an unnecessary multiplicity of the Court proceedings. 3 Accordingly, the application of the Petitioner, E. Jude Marco Perera, is dismissed. JUDGE OF THE SUPREME COURT Mahinda Samayawardhena, J. I agree. JUDGE OF THE SUPREME COURT

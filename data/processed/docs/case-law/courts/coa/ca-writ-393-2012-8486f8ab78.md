The complaint of the Petitioner is that his services were terminated unduly by the Sri Lanka Cricket Board and against the said decision he had made an appeal to the Minister under Section 30 of the Sports Law No. 25 of 1973. The Petitioner complained that he has still not received any positive reply from the said Minister. In the meantime the Petitioner has filed an application in the Labour Tribunal. A settlement was arrived at between the Petitioner and the Cricket Board. In view of the settlement the Petitioner was paid a sum of Rs. 3 million.

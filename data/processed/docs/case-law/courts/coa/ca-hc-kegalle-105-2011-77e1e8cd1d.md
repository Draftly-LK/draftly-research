The Accused-Appellant was convicted of murder of one Galappaththi Gamage Priyantha Galappaththi and sentenced to death. The facts of this case briefly are as follows. The Accused and the deceased had been close friends and also close relatives. According to the evidence of the mother of the deceased both the Accused and the deceased are very close friends and most of the time they eat, drink and sleep and gamble together, and the two of them had no issue as far as she is aware. There was only one eye witness to the incident and he too gave evidence at a very late stage of the trial and he was a person who surrendered after a lapse of time since the version of the prosecution was that the Accused threatened him and he left the village no sooner after the incident, and had been absconding through fear. On the day of the incident the Accused had told this witness Sirisena to give a loaf of bread to his father. He did so but since the father was not at the house he left the loaf of bread on the table.

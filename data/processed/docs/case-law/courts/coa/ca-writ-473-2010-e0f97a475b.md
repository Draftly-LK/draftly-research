The Council shall cause to be kept a book, to be called the "Assessment Book", in which the annual value of each house, building, land or tenement within the Municipality shall be entered every year, and shall cause to be given public notice thereof and the place where the assessment book may be inspected. 2. Every owner or occupier of any house, building, land or tenement, or his authorized agent, shall be permitted free of charge, to inspect any portion of the said assessment book which related to his premises. 3.

Plaintiff Appellant-Absent and unrepresented Anurudhdha Darmaratne with Indika Jayatissa for the Defendant Respondents 24.01.2014 28.04.2014 The Plaintiff Appellant (hereinafter referred to as the Appellant) instituted the said action against the Defendant Respondents (hereinafter referred to as the Respondents) in the District Court of Ratnapura seeking a judgment to partition the land described in the schedule to the plaint. The 3 rd Defendant Respondent has filed a statement of claim praying for a dismissal of the Appellant's action and for a declaration to the land described in the schedule to the plaint. After the framing of issues the learned District Judge has dismissed the Appellant's action on the basis that the land in suit was subject to a charitable trust. Being aggrieved by the said order dated 13.07.2000 the Appellant has appealed to this Court. \ 3 The Appellant has set out several grounds of appeal in paragraph 08 of the petition of appeal. But the Appellant has not shown due diligence in prosecuting this appeal. In the said circumstances I see no reason to interfere with the said order of the learned District Judge dated 13.07.2000. Therefore I dismiss the appeal of the Appellant with costs. Appeal dismissed. Judge of the Court of Appeal I

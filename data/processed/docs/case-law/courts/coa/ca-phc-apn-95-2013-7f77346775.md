Jagath Abeynayake for the Petitioner Rajindra Jayarathne, SC for AG 25.03.2014 The suspect in respect of whom the bail application has been made to the High Court, refused bail despite the fact that he had been on remand by the time of refusal, for one year and six months. However, the fact remains that he has spent a period of well over 2 years and 5 months on remand as at present. The learned State Counsel concedes that according to law he cannot be remanded for more than two years. In the circumstances, we are inclined to accept the revision application and direct the learned Magistrate to grant him cash bail in a sum Rs .100,000/- and surety bail in one million with 2 sureties. Sureties should include the wife and another blood relation. The passport of the suspect to be impounded and in any event if he does not have a passport, the learned Magistrate is directed to inform the Immigration and Emigration Authorities of the travel restriction imposed on the suspect. Suspect is directed to give an undertaking that he will not interfere with the witnesses. report once Keselwaththa a month on the Police Station.

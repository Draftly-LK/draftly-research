M.C.Jayaratne with M.D.J.Bandara for the 1st Defendant￾Respondent 27.03.2013 When this matter was mentioned on the last occasion Mr. Nilantha Kumarage who appeared for the appellant moved for a date to file substitution papers in order to substitute the heirs of the deceased 2nd,3rd and 4th defendant-respondents. Even when this was mentioned on 01.08.2011, it was brought to the notice of Court that the 2nd,3rd and 4th defendant-respondents have expired. Since then no steps have been taken to substitute the heirs of the deceased 2nd,3rd and 4th defendant-respondents. The appellant is absent and unrepresented even today. The above circumstances show that the appellant is not prosecuting this appeal diligently. In the light of the above, Court makes order abating the appeal. Appeal is abated. Accordingly proceedings in this Court are terminated. Proceedings terminated. Judge of the Court of Appeal Kpm/- 1

Counsel for the 30th defendant- appellant moves to support the motion dated 29th January 2014. In that motion, a request being made to have an order directing the Grama Niladharis of the respective areas to issue a certificate indicating the surviving heirs of the deceased 11 th, 12th and 13th defendant-respondents. It is not correct to make an order to a Grama Niladhari to issue such certificates since he is not the person who is capable of ascertaining the heirs of deceased persons. Therefore, the said application is rejected. Learned Counsel also submits that the appellant is not in a position to find out the heirs of the deceased 11th, 12th and 13th defendant-respondents.

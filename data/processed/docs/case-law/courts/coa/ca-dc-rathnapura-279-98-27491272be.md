When this matter was mentioned on the last occas10n namely, 18.12.2012 Counsel for the Appellant informed Court that the 6th Defendant -Respondent is dead. He was then directed to file substitution papers, two weeks before today. When this fact of death of the 6th Defendant was mentioned even on the 18.06.2012, Counsel for the Appellant was directed to file substitution papers. So far no substitution papers have been filed to substitute the heirs of the deceased 6th Defendant. In the circumstances, it is clear that the Appellant is not taking steps to substitute the heirs of the deceased 6th Defendant- - 2- Respondent. Without having the substitution effected, this appeal cannot be taken up for argument. It is the duty of the appellant. Therefore, Court makes an order abating the appeal. Appeal IS abated and therefore, proceedings in this court are terminated. Registrar is directed to keep the original record in this Court for a period of two months from today and then to send the record back to the District Court of Ratnapura for necessary action.

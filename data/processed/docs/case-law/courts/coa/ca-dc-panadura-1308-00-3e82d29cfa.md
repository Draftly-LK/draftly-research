According to the minutes dated 28.01.2014 the death certificate of the plaintiff-appellant which has been sent by the wife of the plaintiff-appellant has been filed of record. It appears that the heirs of the deceased appellant have not shown due diligence in taking necessary steps for the purpose of prosecuting this appeal. In the said circumstances, I dismiss the appeal of the appellant without costs. Registrar is directed to send the main case record to the relevant District Court. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL Kwk/=

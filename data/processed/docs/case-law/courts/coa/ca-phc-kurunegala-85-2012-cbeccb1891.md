# ca phc kurunegala 85 2012

IN THE COURT OF APPEAL OF THE DEMOCRATIC SOCIALIST REPUBLIC OF SRI LANKA CA (PHC) 85/2012 PHC-KURUNEGALA HCR 44-2008 Mohomed Safayar Mohomed Budumutthawa, Nikaweratiya. Appellant Vs. Wanninayake Mudiyanselage Chandana Kumara Wanninayake Divisional Secretory (Acting), Nikaweratiya. Respondent CA (PHC) 85/2012 PHC-KURUNEGALA HCR 44-2008 BEFORE: COUNSEL: ARGUED & DECIDED ON: A.W.A. Salam, J. A.W.A. Salam, J. & W.M.M. Malinie Gunaratne, J. M. Jayasinghe SC for the Respondent. 13.03.2014. This is an appeal preferred against the judgment of the learned High Court Judge who exercised the revisionary jurisdiction in respect of an order made by the learned Magistrate under the State Land (Recovery of Possession) Act. As it has been submitted by the learned State Counsel the land in question is a State Land and therefore in terms of the Judgment of the Supreme Court in SC Appeal 21/2013 this appeal cannot be maintained. Accordingly the appeal stands dismissed. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL. W.M.M. MaUnie Gunaratne, J. I agree. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL. Mm/-.

The Defendant Appellant, Peoples Bank is absent and unrepresented. 20.03.2014 On several dates counsel for the Defendant Appellant had moved for time to take steps for the substitution of the deceased plaintiff-Respondent in this case. This court is of the view that the Defendant Appellant has not shown due diligence to prosecute this appeal. Therefore, this court makes order abating the appeal. Appeal is abated.

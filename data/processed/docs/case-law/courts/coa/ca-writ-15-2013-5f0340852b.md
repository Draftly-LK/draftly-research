The Petitioner in this application is seeking a Writ of Certiorari to quash the document marked P8. P8 is an order/findings of the Court of Inquiry dated 04.12.2012. In the said Court of Inquiry the Petitioner himself has stated that he accept the responsibility for the entire amount that was not brought into the account of the said programme funds and therefore he agrees to pay the balance money Rs. 1,424,500.00 that is due. This is an admission by the Petitioner in the said Court of Inquiry and the Court of Inquiry has arrived at the findings on the said admission. A Writ of Certiorari to quash that order will not arise in these 2 circumstances. Therefore, this Court refuses to issue notice on the Respondents. P.W.D.C. Jayathilake I agree. Mm/-.

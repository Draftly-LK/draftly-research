(Gampaha) case No: - 145/2006 Wellangahagoda Gedara Roshan Wajira Kumara (Prisoner in remand custody) Accused - Petitioner Vs. The Officer-in-charge Police Station Nittabuwa. Hon. Attorney General Attorney General's Department Colombo 12. Respondents C.A. (PHC) APN No. 122/2013 H.C. Gampaha 145/2006 BEFORE COUNSEL ARGUED & DECIDED ON A.W. A SALAM J. A.W.A. Salam J. Sunil Rajapakshe J. Jeffry Zainudeen for the Petitioner. Rajindra Jayaratne SC. for the Respondents. 24.02.2014 Heard both parties for and against the application. The 2nd accused admittedly was serving a period of imprisonment during the period of 11 years, he claims he was on remand. This as contended by the learned State Counsel is a non-disclosure of the accused having served a term of imprisonment pending his trial relating to the commission of offences set out in the indictment. As the accused has failed to invoke the jurisdiction with clean hands, we are of the opinion that he is not entitled to be heard on this application. Accordingly, we dismiss this application. JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEAL SUNIL RAJAPAKSHE J. I agree ,JUDGE OF THE COURT OF APPEA CN/- 1

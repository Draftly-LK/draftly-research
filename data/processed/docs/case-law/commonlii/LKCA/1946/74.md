Where a landlord lets premises to partners carrying on business under a firm name a notice to quite addressed to the name of the firm and not to the individual partners would be valid and sufficient notice. The Commissioner of Requests entered judgment for the plaintiff but directed that the writ of ejectment should not issue till August 31, 1946. The defendants have appealed from that judgment. Two questions arise for determination on this appeal :- (1) Is the notice to quit (Dl of July 16, 1945) given by the plaintiff a valid and sufficient notice? I am unable to uphold that contention. The evidence led by the defence shows that all the defendants were aware of the notice and, in fact, that they all asked their Proctor to send a reply to that notice en July 20, 1945. Moreover, under our law a firm is not a separate and distinct legal persona. The firm name is a conventional name applicable to the persons who are partners of the firm at the time when the name is used.

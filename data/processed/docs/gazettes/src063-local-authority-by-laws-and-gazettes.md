# Local Authority By-Laws and Gazettes

## Purpose

Local-authority requirements depend on the property's municipal, urban
council, or pradeshiya sabha area. This collection is a case-selection index,
not a claim that one national by-law applies everywhere.

- [Model Standard By-Laws for Pradeshiya Sabhas](official-model-by-laws/model-standard-by-laws-pradeshiya-sabhas-en.pdf)
- [Model By-Laws on Solid Waste for Municipal Councils](official-model-by-laws/model-by-laws-solid-waste-municipal-councils-en.pdf)

- [Department of Government Printing gazette archive](https://www.documents.gov.lk/view/gazettes/)

These are official Ministry model instruments. They establish the reusable
baseline, but they do not prove that a specific council has adopted an
instrument unchanged.

## Case Selection

- Identify the relevant local authority from the property's address and
 assessment records.
- Check the authority's official notices and the Department of Government
 Printing gazette archive for current by-laws, declarations, and procedures.
- Keep the local-authority name, source URL, gazette number, publication date,
 and a local copy in the case manifest before relying on the material.

## Verification Rule

The model by-laws and three statutes establish the national framework. For an
individual conveyancing matter, record the property's local authority and
verify the council's adoption, modification, or replacement notice in the
official gazette archive before treating a by-law as applicable.
